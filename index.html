About

Milan‑based creative consultancy with an international network. We work with design studios, brands, and institutions — bringing strategy, connections, and creative direction to every project. Between brief and delivery — close enough to understand both sides, experienced enough to get it right.

Curation

We don't scroll through contacts. Every introduction we make is deliberate — based on knowing who you are, what your project needs, and who in our network can actually deliver.

Trust

Our network is built on years of working side by side, not on a list. The people we connect you with know us, and know we don't make introductions lightly.

Execution

Strategy without follow-through is just a document. We stay close to a project from brief to delivery, making sure decisions made at the table translate into results on the ground.
