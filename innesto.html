A cultural project by Yapa

Innesto is a nomad format that brings designers, artists, brands and creatives into the spaces of the city. Not a gallery, not a fair — an innesto. The work meets the people who inhabit it, in living, informal, unexpected environments. Each edition is a curated selection by Yapa: chosen for the rigour of their process and the strength of the piece.
